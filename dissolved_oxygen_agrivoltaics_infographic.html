1. Why Dissolved Oxygen Matters

Dissolved Oxygen (DO) is critical for root respiration. Roots require oxygen for aerobic metabolism, ATP production, nutrient uptake, and disease resistance. Insufficient DO leads to hypoxia, reduced nutrient absorption, and root rot.

Optimal Hydroponic Range: 6–9 mg/L

3. Temperature vs Oxygen Solubility

Oxygen solubility decreases as temperature increases. Agrivoltaic shading affects nutrient temperature and therefore DO availability.

4. Agrivoltaic System Impacts

PV Shading

Reduces solar heating → Potentially increases oxygen solubility.

Microclimate

Higher humidity and altered airflow influence nutrient temperature stability.

Dynamic Shading

Diurnal radiation shifts may create DO oscillations.

5. Sensor Requirements for R&D

  • Type: Optical (Luminescent)
  • Range: 0–20 mg/L
  • Accuracy: ±0.1–0.3 mg/L
  • Resolution: 0.01 mg/L
  • Compensation: Temperature (mandatory), Salinity (preferred)
  • Output: RS485 (Modbus RTU) preferred for ESP32 integration

6. Under R4000 Sensor Strategy

Recommended Architecture:

  • Optical DO probe (RS485)
  • ESP32 microcontroller
  • MAX485 transceiver module
  • 12V regulated supply
  • LoRa transmission layer

Expected real-world accuracy in this budget: ±0.3 mg/L

7. Monitoring Strategy

  • Log DO + Temperature + EC + pH + PAR
  • Sampling interval: 1–5 minutes
  • Install in flowing section (avoid dead zones)
  • Regular cleaning to prevent biofouling
